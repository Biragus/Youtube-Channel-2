## Description

**You know something. Somebody out there would pay to learn it. This guide shows you how to turn it into a product, and how to let Claude, an AI assistant, do the heavy lifting for you.**

You've seen the stories on the channel: someone at zero, broke, fired, rejected, takes something they already know, puts it into a simple file, and sells it to the people who need it.

This is the part the stories skip. The exact steps, plus 16 copy-paste prompts that do them for you.

**Inside the guide (31 pages, 11 chapters, 16 Claude prompts):**

- **Find what hurts.** A simple method for finding an idea people will actually pay for, using your own job, problems and experience.
- **Choose the format.** Template, tracker, guide, checklist or kit: match the product to the problem.
- **Check it before you build it.** The 10-conversation test, with the exact message to send, so you don't waste a month on something nobody wants.
- **Build it in a weekend.** A two-day plan using free tools (Google Docs, Sheets, Canva, Notion).
- **Price it.** What to charge, and how to set a launch price.
- **Open your shop.** Set up a selling page and take your first payment.
- **Your first ten sales.** The 30-second video formula and 12 video ideas, no audience needed.
- **When it goes wrong.** What to do about zero sales, refunds, angry comments and mistakes.
- **Product two and three.** Turn one product into a small shop.
- **Do it all with Claude.** A step-by-step checklist with 16 copy-paste prompts: find your idea, write and design the product, make your shop images and page, then create animated story videos to sell it. No coding or design skills needed.

**This is for you if:**

- You have a job, skill, hobby or problem you've solved, and you think "nobody would pay for that".
- You have little or no money to start. The core steps work with free tools; the video steps use paid tools (Claude and ElevenLabs) that cost a few dollars per video.
- You have no audience. You need ten buyers, not ten thousand followers.

**This is not for you if** you're looking for a get-rich-quick shortcut. This is a practical method, and it takes work.

**FAQ**

*What format is it?* A PDF, delivered instantly after purchase. It works on phone, tablet and computer, and you can copy the prompts straight out of it.

*Do I need any experience?* No. It's written for complete beginners.

*Do I need to spend money on tools?* Not for the product itself. For the videos you'll want a paid Claude plan and some ElevenLabs credits.

*Do I need to know how to code?* No. You copy a prompt, paste it into Claude and answer its questions.

*Will I make money?* That depends on your product and your effort, and nobody can honestly promise you a result. This guide gives you a clear method and helps you avoid the most common mistakes.

*Refunds?* If the guide isn't what you expected, email within 14 days and you'll get a full refund.
