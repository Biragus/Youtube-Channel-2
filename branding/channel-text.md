# Channel text

## Description (paste into YouTube)

Everybody loves the after. This channel is about the before.

Every week, one animated story about someone who started with nothing: broke, fired, rejected, laughed at. And the small, unglamorous steps that got them out.

No gurus. No "get rich in 30 days". Just the part nobody shows you: the first failed attempt, the moment they nearly quit, and the one question that changed everything.

If you are at zero right now, you are in the right place.

New story every week. Subscribe so you do not miss the next one.

The stories are fictional and for motivation and entertainment. They are not financial or career advice.
