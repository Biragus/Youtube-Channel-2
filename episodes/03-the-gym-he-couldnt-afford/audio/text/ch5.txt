What if you made the plan so easy that you could not fail it?

Day one. Ten squats, holding the kitchen counter. That is it.

It feels ridiculous. You do it anyway, and you put a tick in the notebook.

Day two. Ten squats and a five-minute walk around the block. Tick.

You add one small thing each week. Never two.

Wall push-ups. Then stairs, once, slowly. Then twice.

You swap the vending machine for a banana and a sandwich from home. Not every day. Most days.

Some weeks you miss three days in a row. The old you would have quit. This time, you just start again on the next page.

You write down everything. What you did. How you felt. What hurt. What helped.

Without knowing it, you are writing a map. The map you wished someone had given you.

Week six. You climb all fourteen stairs without stopping.

Nobody claps. Nobody is there. But you stand at the top for a long time anyway.
