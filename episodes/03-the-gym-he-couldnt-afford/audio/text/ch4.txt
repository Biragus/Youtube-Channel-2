So you find a free workout video online. Thirty minutes. Full body.

You make it four minutes.

The next day, you cannot walk down the stairs without holding the rail. Again.

You try again on Thursday. Then the next Monday. Then you stop.

Each time you stop, you feel a little worse than before you started.

Every plan you find is made by people who already love exercise, for people who already love exercise.

Nobody makes a plan for someone who is starting from the floor.

And lying there, you have an idea that sounds almost too small to matter.
