And then, they come back.

Dex hurts his back lifting too heavy. His physio tells him to start again from the basics.

The page the physio shows him is from your plan.

And then an email arrives from the big gym on the corner.

Their members keep quitting in February. They want a beginner programme. They have heard yours is the one people actually finish.

You walk back through the glass doors. Brick is at the desk, with the same clipboard.

He does not recognise you.

You put a single page on the counter. Your old gym quote. Sixty dollars a month, twelve-month contract.

"I couldn't afford this," you say. "So I built something for the people you turn away."

Then you sit down, and you name your price.

He reads the number twice. Then he signs.
