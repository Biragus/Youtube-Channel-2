One year after you couldn't climb fourteen stairs, thirty thousand people have used your plan.

You have lost eighteen kilos. Slowly. Your doctor high-fives you, which you did not know doctors did.

The hoodie goes into a drawer.

You hire Sam to answer the messages. He still does the stairs every morning.

And you take your mom on a walk along the beach. The one you stopped going to.

She does not say anything. She just holds your arm a little tighter.
