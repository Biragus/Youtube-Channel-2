Two days later, you are back in the same building. Delivering their lunch order.

The lift doors close, and you are standing next to a woman with a lanyard.

She looks at you. "You interviewed on Thursday."

She is a recruiter. Her name is Nora. She looks very tired.

And because it is a long lift ride, she tells you the truth.

"Nobody read your résumé. Not a person, anyway."

"A piece of software reads it first. If it cannot find the right words, we never even see you."

"And when a human does look, you get a few seconds. That is all anybody gets."

"You are not being rejected. You are not being seen."

The doors open. She walks out. You forget to get off.

You stand there holding six sandwiches, trying to work out how many of your hundred were ever opened.

Two years. One hundred applications. And you were playing a game nobody told you the rules of.
