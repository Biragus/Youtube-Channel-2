And then, they come back.

Dex's company goes under. He asks, quietly, if the kit works for people like him.

You send it to him for free. And you do not make a joke.

Uncle Ray's son cannot find a job. Suddenly, the theory changes.

And then an email arrives from a company you recognise.

They are losing good candidates. They want someone to train their recruiters. They have heard you are the best.

You walk into the same glass office. Nora is in the front row. She is smiling.

Mr. Graves is in the second row. He does not recognise you.

You put one slide on the screen. A rejection email. Dated a Thursday. Signed by him.

You give him four seconds to read it. You count.

Nobody in the room breathes.

Then you click to the next slide, and you teach them how to actually see people.
