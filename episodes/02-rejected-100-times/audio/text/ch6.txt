You go back to stacking tins, and you try not to hope.

Day three. Your phone buzzes in aisle nine.

An interview.

Then another. Then two more.

Ten applications. Four interviews. After one hundred, and zero.

You drop a tin of beans on your foot. You do not even feel it.

Three weeks later, you have a job offer. A real one. With a desk.

Your mom cries. Uncle Ray says it was about time.

And that could have been the end of the story.

But on your last night shift, Sam is crying in the stock room.

He got laid off from his day job. He has sent sixty applications. Nothing.

You sit down on the crate next to him, and you open your laptop.

You show him the word lists. You rewrite his first page with him, line by line.

Two weeks later, Sam has three interviews.

And that is when you understand what you are actually holding.
