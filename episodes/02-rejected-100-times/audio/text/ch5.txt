That night, you do not sleep. You open the spreadsheet again.

But this time, you are not looking at it like a list of failures.

You are looking at it like data.

One hundred job adverts. One hundred versions of your résumé. One hundred results.

Nobody else on earth has this spreadsheet.

A hundred failures, perfectly organised. It might be the most useful thing you own.

So you start reading the adverts the way the software reads them.

The same words keep showing up. The words they are actually searching for.

Your résumé does not contain a single one of them.

You wrote, hard worker. They were searching for, inventory management.

You stocked forty thousand items a week on a night shift. That is inventory management.

You were never unqualified. You were untranslated.

So you rewrite everything. One page. Their words. Your numbers at the top.

It takes you nine nights. You do it on your breaks, sitting on a crate of tinned tomatoes.

And then you send out ten.
