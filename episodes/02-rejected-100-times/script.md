# Episode 02 — Rejected 100 Times

- **Target length:** about 9 minutes. 121 shots, one per narration line.
- **Narration:** Charliee, speaking to "you". Character lines appear as speech bubbles and are read by the narrator.
- **Hero:** same look as episode 01 (black head, neon green eyes, spiky hair). Grey hoodie until chapter 9, navy suit after.
- **New character:** Nora, a tired recruiter (short dark hair, teal blazer, lanyard). Returning cast: Mom, Uncle Ray, Dex, Sam, Mr. Graves (now a hiring manager).
- **Recurring devices:** a rejection counter in the corner (climbs to 100), a wall of rejection emails, and from chapter 7 a sales counter.
- **Music:** tense intro (hook), heavy and sad (ch. 2–4), curious (ch. 5–6), driving build (ch. 7–8), warm (ch. 9–11). Same mix levels as episode 01.
- **Call to action:** only in the final line.

Columns: shot · what the narrator says · what we see · sound.
