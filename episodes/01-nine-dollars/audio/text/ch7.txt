You build it in six nights.

You have no idea how to make a sales page. You make one anyway. It is ugly.

And then, with your heart pounding, you press publish.

And you wait for the money to roll in.

Day one. Nothing. Day five. Nothing.

Day twelve. One visitor. It is your mom.

Dex sends you a screenshot of your page, with three laughing faces.

The rent counter says eighteen days. Your balance says eleven dollars.

Everything inside you says stop. And this is the exact moment where most people do.

Your finger is on the delete button.

But instead, you ask the one question that changes everything. Why is nobody buying?
