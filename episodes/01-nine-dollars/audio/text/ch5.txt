And that is exactly when the real problem arrives.

Monday morning, Mr. Graves calls every driver to the loading bay.

The company has bought a new routing system. It only needs half of you.

He reads the badge numbers out loud, one at a time, like a shopping list.

Four four seven one.

"Nothing personal. You were never going to be more than this anyway."

You carry your things out in a cardboard box. The same kind you used to deliver.

When you get home, there is a notice taped to your door.

Forty-three dollars. No job. Thirty days to pay the rent.

You sit down on the floor, with your back against the door.

And you realise something strange. For the first time in your life, you have nothing left to lose.
