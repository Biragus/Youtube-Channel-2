And one morning, you notice something. You did not check your bank balance.

Eighteen months after the loading bay, sixty thousand drivers use something that you made.

The grey hoodie goes into a drawer.

You move somewhere with windows. Somewhere you cannot touch both walls.

For a while, you just stand in the middle of the room, and listen to how quiet it is.

The first person you hire is Sam.

And you pay off the house your mom has been renting for thirty years.

She does not say anything. She just holds the key, and cries.
