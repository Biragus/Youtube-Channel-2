You find the videos again.

But this time, you do not just watch them. You study them, with a pen in your hand.

The library has free internet and a heater. So you basically move in.

You learn the first rule. Do not sell what you like. Sell what hurts.

So you ask yourself a different question. Who do you actually know? And what hurts them?

Drivers. Forty of them just lost their jobs with you. Thousands more work for themselves.

And every single one of them hates the same three things. Tracking miles. Tracking fuel. And tax.

You know that pain. You tracked your own numbers in a notebook for five years.

So what if that notebook was a simple spreadsheet that any driver could use?

You give it a name. The Driver's Ledger. And you give it a price. Nine dollars.

For the first time, the idea is not a dream. It is a file, on your screen, with your name on it.

It is not much. But it is the first thing you have ever made that belongs to you.
