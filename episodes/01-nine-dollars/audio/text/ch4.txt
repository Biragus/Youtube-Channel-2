You are a van driver. What could you possibly sell to anybody?

Still, the idea will not leave you alone. So at lunch, you mention it to Dex.

Dex laughs so hard he drops his sandwich.

"Bro. You? Selling files on the internet? To who?"

You tell your mom. She goes very quiet.

"Honey, those things are scams. Please. Just keep the job."

Uncle Ray does not even look up from his plate. "Real men have real jobs."

Nobody is being cruel. They honestly believe they are protecting you.

But the loudest voice is not any of theirs. It is the one inside your own head.

It will never work. Not for someone like you.

So you delete the videos. You set your alarm. And you go back to work.
