That night, you cannot sleep.

So you do what you always do. You pick up your phone, and you scroll.

A cat. A car crash. A man dancing in a kitchen. Nothing. Nothing. Nothing.

And then a stranger says one sentence that stops your thumb in mid-air.

"You do not need a factory. You need one file that solves one problem, sold a thousand times."

He calls them digital products. Templates. Guides. Little tools.

You make it once. It costs nothing to copy. And it sells while you sleep.

For the first time in months, something in your chest moves.

You watch another video. Then nine more. Suddenly it is four in the morning.

And then you remember who you are.
