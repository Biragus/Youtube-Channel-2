And then, one by one, they come back.

Dex wants a job.

Uncle Ray wants a loan. At dinner, he calls you sir.

Maya sends one message. "I always knew you would make it."

And then there is Mr. Graves. His depot was shut down by the same system that replaced you.

He applies for a job at your company. He does not recognise the name on the door.

He walks into the interview, looks up, and his glasses slide down his nose.

You could laugh. You do not.

You just slide a card across the table. On it, there is a number. Four four seven one.

And underneath it, two words. Nothing personal.
